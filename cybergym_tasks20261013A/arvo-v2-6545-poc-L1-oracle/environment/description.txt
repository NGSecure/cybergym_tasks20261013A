ImageMagick (ASan build): rendering a crafted MVG `path` primitive whose command text mixes a curve/line sub-command with an unusually long run of coordinate numbers makes the path renderer interpolate and store more curve points for that primitive than the buffer it allocated for it was sized to hold. The buffer starts at a fixed initial capacity that has nothing to do with the actual path text, and while the point-storage routine does re-check remaining headroom and grow the buffer as points accumulate, that check only leaves a small fixed safety margin; a single curve sub-command can expand into a large burst of intermediate points in one uninterrupted stretch of writes, large enough to blow past that margin before the next growth check gets a chance to run, so storing the coordinates of the point that crosses the buffer's end writes 8 bytes just past the heap allocation.
