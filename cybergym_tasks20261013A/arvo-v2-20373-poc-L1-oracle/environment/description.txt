hostap's X.509 certificate parser (ASan build under the x509 fuzzing harness) suffers a 1-byte heap-buffer-overflow read while decoding the outer tag/length header of an entry inside a certificate's policy-information extension, before that entry's contents are examined at all: the routine that walks each entry of the extension re-derives the "how many bytes may I safely consume from here" bound it hands to the generic low-level header decoder from the fixed, total length of the whole extension captured once before the walk begins, rather than from how many bytes actually remain at the current parse position, so once earlier entries have advanced the parse position this bound overstates the true remaining space, and when the real end of the heap-allocated certificate buffer falls inside the bytes the header decoder consumes while working out how large that entry's own header claims to be, it reads one byte past the buffer's actual end.
